How do I complete a garden?

Place plants on available plots. Connect the water source to the flowers through plants that carry water. Bloom the goals before your moves run out.

What happens if I lose?

You can restart the garden. Progress already earned, stars and coins remain saved on this device.

Do I need hints?

No. All 50 gardens can be solved without hints. In-game coins and hints are virtual and do not require real-money purchases.

Why might an ad appear?

The game is free and may show one ad when it opens, depending on ad availability and your privacy choices. Ads are not required to play.

How do I change my privacy choices?

Where required in your region, open “Privacy” in the home screen credits. On iPhone, you can also manage tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
