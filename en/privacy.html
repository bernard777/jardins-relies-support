Your game data

The Connected Gardens is a free game with no user account or real-money purchases. Your progress, stars, coins, hints, settings and current game are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this progress on a game server. Uninstalling the app may erase this local data.

Ads and consent

The game uses Google AdMob to show an app-open ad when one is available. Google and its partners may process device or app identifiers, approximate location inferred in part from IP address, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic and performance data. This data may be used to serve, measure and improve ads and, where you allow it, to personalize ads or track activity across apps and sites. The game requests applicable consent choices before requesting an ad. On iOS, it also asks for Apple's tracking permission before accessing the advertising identifier. Declining does not prevent you from playing.

For more about Google's processing, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

Where required in your region, the “Privacy” entry in the game's home screen credits lets you revisit ad choices. On iOS, tracking permission can be changed in device settings. You can also remove local game data by uninstalling the app. For data processed by Google, consult its privacy policy and controls.

Support and contact

If you email ndongojba@gmail.com, we use your message and address to answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information. You can request deletion of the exchange through the same address.
